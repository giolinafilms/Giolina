## Google Business Profile: legitimate recommendation only

Official policy reviewed: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en  and https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en .

Google permits a service-area business that travels to customers. Use one profile tied to the genuine operating location; hide the address when customers are not served there. A mailing/virtual address is not an eligible substitute. Public storefront addresses require real customer reception and signage; co-working eligibility adds business staffing. Multiple profiles require genuinely separate staffed locations. Therefore do not create or promote a Madison storefront from the family address. If Madison is not the genuine operating base, hiding it does not make it eligible: first establish the actual base with the owner. Keep real-world brand name, without keyword/city stuffing.

Google allows up to 20 specifically named service areas (cities, postal codes or other accepted areas), not a radius. Overall coverage should generally remain within about two hours' driving time of the real base. Select actual service locations, not all researched counties. Nothing here authorizes listing creation or changes.

Local ranking guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en . Google describes relevance, distance and prominence; no guaranteed ranking from expanding service areas or changing website language. Website organic strategy and eligible GBP positioning are distinct.

Review guidance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en . Ask real clients for honest reviews without incentives or selecting only satisfied clients. This plan proposes owner follow-through, not automated messaging or sending requests now.

## Practical 60–90-day organic plan (no ads)

Days 1–14: Record a baseline before changes: Search Console clicks/impressions/CTR by page and query, indexed pages, current enquiry count and sources, qualified leads, consultations and bookings. If analytics access is unavailable, mark baseline unknown; do not report zero. Clarify real operating location separately from served market. Audit homepage/services/contact copy for North Jersey priority and remove unsupported office/venue claims. Confirm rights and contextual facts for the two films.

Days 15–30: Publish or prepare for owner approval two strong film-first case studies above, linked from the relevant service pages and portfolio. Add unique headings/titles, visible couple/venue facts, accurate poster dimensions, and one clear date/venue enquiry CTA. Use a prominent discoverable video embed with fallback watch link; avoid video discovery that depends solely on a user click. Use truthful video metadata only. Verify page/canonical/indexability and poster/video rendering on mobile. Google's video guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video ; indexing is not guaranteed even with markup.

Days 31–60: Improve one North Jersey service/portfolio hub using the two existing cases, with NYC secondary. Add at most one or two further case studies only when real work, rights and details are confirmed. Record county/venue as actual CRM lead attributes. Owner can ask actual past couples for honest reviews and offer relevant collaborators an approved case-study link; no bulk outreach, bought links, or unsolicited messages sent by this research task. Assess existing directory consistency without publishing changes.

Days 61–90: Compare equal 28-day windows plus year-over-year context if available; annotate weddings' seasonal demand and low sample sizes. Review query impressions and clicks for brand/nonbrand, North Jersey and the two venues separately. Improve a page's title/intro if relevant impressions occur with low CTR; improve case proof/CTA if visits yield weak enquiries. Diagnose indexability before creating more pages if impressions remain absent. Extend geography only when enquiries or real portfolio evidence supports it. No town-page factory and no Staten Island venue claims.

Measurement: verified enquiry submissions (not button clicks) → qualified leads → consultation → booking; retain landing page, source/medium, requested venue and couple-origin location separately. Also track phone/email clicks as microconversions, film engagement where feasible, form delivery reliability and response time. Avoid PII in analytics. Weekly check for broken forms/pages; monthly trend review. Set business targets after the baseline; no invented lead forecast or 90-day ranking promise.
