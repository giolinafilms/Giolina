# Optional analytics notice — activation draft

Status: prepared for review; not a published privacy policy. Production tracking remains disabled. The proposed notice below describes the consent-controlled implementation, not the unexplained recent activity reported in GA4.

## Proposed visitor notice

GioLina Films uses optional Google Analytics cookies to understand visits to its public website and improve its pages. Analytics starts only after you select Accept Analytics. Declining or ignoring the choice leaves this analytics implementation off and does not prevent browsing or inquiries.

With your permission, this implementation sends public page addresses without query parameters or fragments, page titles, the origin of an external referring website, and interaction categories such as Contact, email, telephone and consultation-link clicks. These clicks indicate interest; they are not confirmed inquiries. Custom events do not include your name, email address, phone number, message, form contents or clicked destination. Google also processes technical request information, including browser, device and network information. Analytics is not described as anonymous.

Google provides the analytics service. Google Signals is off and user-provided data collection is not configured. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization remain denied. We do not enable advertising personalization through this implementation.

The Analytics property is configured to retain event-level and user-level data for 14 months. New activity resets the retention period for the user identifier; it does not restart the age of every historical event. Standard aggregated reports are not subject to this same retention setting. Cookie lifetimes and the browser's consent preference are separate from this server-side retention period.

Your acceptance or decline is stored on this browser for six months. You can change your choice using Analytics Preferences in the footer. Withdrawing stops future tracking from this implementation, removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page without analytics. It does not automatically erase information already processed by Google. A supported Global Privacy Control signal keeps analytics off.

The website's Contact form sends inquiries through Formspree. Consultation links lead to the existing scheduling service. Inquiry handling is separate from optional analytics: rejecting analytics does not prevent you from contacting us. This analytics notice does not establish an inquiry-retention period or claim to replace the inquiry providers' notices.

For privacy questions or requests, contact GioLina Films at info@giolina.co.
