# Optional analytics notice — DRAFT, not approved for production

GioLina Films proposes optional Google Analytics 4 to understand visits to its public website and improve its pages. Analytics would start only after you select Accept Analytics. Declining or ignoring the choice would leave analytics off and would not prevent browsing or inquiries.

When enabled with your permission, Google Analytics would receive the public page address without query parameters or fragments, page title, a sanitized external referring-site origin, and selected interaction categories such as telephone, email, Contact and consultation-link clicks. The website would not send the destination email address, phone number, customer names or form contents in these custom events. Google also processes technical information associated with requests, such as browser/device and network information. Do not describe this as anonymous or as collecting no personal data.

Google provides the analytics service. Advertising consent would remain denied, with Google Signals and advertising personalization off. Optional analytics cookies would be used after consent. A first-party preference would remember acceptance or decline for six months. Analytics Preferences in the footer would allow you to change the choice. Withdrawal would stop future tracking, remove accessible first-party GA cookies and reload without analytics. It would not automatically erase information already processed by Google. A supported Global Privacy Control signal would keep analytics off.

Contact GioLina Films at info@giolina.co with questions about this proposal. Existing inquiry services are separate; this draft does not claim to describe every provider or all inquiry-data practices.
